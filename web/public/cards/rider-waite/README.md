# Rider–Waite–Smith minor arcana

These 56 images reproduce the original 1910 “Pam-A” Rider–Waite–Smith deck illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith (1878–1951), under the direction of Arthur Edward Waite. They are original-deck scans, not a modern copyrighted recoloring.

Source set: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite-Smith_tarot_deck_(TaionWC)

Each original file's source page, image URL, description, artist metadata, and public-domain designation are recorded in `sources.json`. The source pages identify the original publication and mark the images public domain, including in the United States and the source country. Example: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cups01.jpg

Local preparation: Wikimedia Commons 250-pixel thumbnails, resized proportionally to fit 180 × 310 pixels, encoded as WebP at quality 80. No recoloring or generative modification. The 56 gameplay images total 1,179,900 bytes.

Mapping: Swords/Cups/Pentacles/Wands use suit indices 0/1/2/3. Files use ranks 01–14: Ace, 2–10, Page, Knight, Queen, King. Tarot aces score 1 or 11; numbered cards retain their number; all four court ranks score 10. The artwork does not change those values.

The complete minor-arcana family is mixed with the complete conventional 52-card family and 21 original parody cards. It is not a 20% replacement skin or an additional face-card-only pack.

## Major-arcana cheat art

The additional `Major00.webp` through `Major21.webp` assets are the 22 original major arcana from the same public-domain Pam-A scan set. Their individual provenance and public-domain designations are recorded in `major-sources.json`. They use the same proportional WebP conversion and total 517,322 bytes. They are sideboard-only special effects, not additional cards in the 129-card draw deck. Their printed arcana numbers are not blackjack hand values.
