# Frick-frack yellow-flag backdrop

`frick-frack-yellow-flags.webp` is the clean, user-approved generated picnic-table
background (2026-10-31), corrected to plain cloth and rectangular wood poles, encoded from the 1672 × 941 original PNG at WebP quality
88. It contains only scenery: blanket, ground, and full-size rolled yellow flags.
It also includes rough quartz and one cheap black plastic sunglasses pair near the edges.
There are no baked cards, dice, deck, counters, labels, buttons, or item shadows.

The shared Three renderer draws this cosmetic image beneath real card meshes,
the host-result die, and the live deck. Accessible controls and numeric status
remain live DOM UI. Never put authoritative gameplay state into this asset.

`frick-frack-yellow-flags-portrait.webp` is the separately composed 887 × 1774
portrait variant of the same scene. The renderer selects it for tall panels so
landscape flag poles are not squeezed into a phone layout. Both variants retain
an empty central playing area; decorative props have no gameplay interaction.
